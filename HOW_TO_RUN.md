# Wealth & Tax Planner - How to Access

## Live App URL
**https://freelorine.web.app**

## How to Access
1. Open your browser and go to: **https://freelorine.web.app**
2. Click **"Sign in with Google"**
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. The app will load automatically

## Who Can Access
Only authorized Google accounts:
- jammy.pate@gmail.com

If you try to sign in with a different email, you will see an "Access denied" message.

## Troubleshooting

### "Failed to sign in"
- Make sure you're using an authorized Google account
- Check that you have a stable internet connection
- Try refreshing the page and signing in again

### Redirect loop or blank page
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try an incognito/private window
- Make sure popups are allowed for this site
